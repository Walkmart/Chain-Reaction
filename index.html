How to Play

  1. Type directly into the tiles; correct letters turn green and lock.
  2. Each adjacent pair forms a single closed-compound word.
  3. You get 6 tries per word. Hint reveals one correct letter (but never the last).
  4. Scoring: +10 per word, −10 per hint, −5 per wrong check, +20 bonus for finishing the chain.
  5. One chain per day. Results save to your account if signed in.
